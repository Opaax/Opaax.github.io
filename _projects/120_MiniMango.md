### Context

This is my first **internship as programmer**. For **six months** from **February to August 2021**.<br>
At Mini Mango, I worked on the children’s educational game **Yummy Restaurant**.<br>
I also, briefly contributed to the **Spaceship project**, focusing on debugging before the release.

### My Work

I Designed the game flow and implemented cooking mechanics (cutting, peeling, washing, baking, mixing, feeding the hero).
Briefly contributed to the Spaceship project, focusing on debugging before release.
Integrated various SDKs to enhance gameplay features.
Maintained and updated iOS builds via XCode, ensuring compliance with frequent regulation changes for children’s apps.

---

### Conclusion

It was a fantastic internship that taught me a great deal about mobile game development, particularly the regulations and design considerations when targeting a young audience.
Many thanks to my lead for their support and insightful advice.
