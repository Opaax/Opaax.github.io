Who I am

Hi I'm Enguerran COBERT.
I grew up in Savoie, France, a land of snowy peaks, melted cheese, and winding mountain trails.
When I wasn’t outside exploring, I was inside, captivated by the worlds my brother explored through video games. That’s where it all began, my fascination with how games come to life.

My Journey

I first studied domestic and industrial electricity, where I learned to appreciate systems, precision, and logic.
But my creative side led me toward Game Art, where I dove into 3D modeling, animation, and texturing.
It was during that time, while integrating my art into Unity through Playmaker, that I discovered C#, and instantly fell in love with the craft of programming.
Eager to grow, I joined Isart Digital Paris, where I deepened my skills in game programming and game design, learning how to turn ideas into interactive, playable experiences.

Today

After graduating, I chose to settle in Montréal, Canada 🇨🇦, a place that feels both creative and wild, where technology and nature coexist. Québec’s open landscapes and vibrant gaming scene have become my source of freedom, focus, and inspiration.
